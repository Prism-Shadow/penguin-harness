# Reading notes

## Before you start

When the request only names the skill, ask which book or reading goal to work on. If the reader has not supplied a notebook or passages, ask them to open Reading Desk from the App Center, open the notebook, and export the Markdown file. The shelf and annotations live in their browser. Ask them to attach the exported file or paste the passages they want to discuss.

## Preparing a guide

Keep the book title and chapter headings from the notebook. Treat quoted passages as source text and the reader’s notes as their interpretation. When building a chapter guide, use a short summary, the reader’s questions, and one or two recall prompts per chapter. Ground each answer in an included passage and flag questions the provided text leaves open.

Follow the reader’s language. Preserve a quotation in its original language when explaining it in another.

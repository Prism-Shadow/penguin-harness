          HOW IT WORKS

          How to use it

          1. Add a picture. Drag it in, click to choose it or paste it with Ctrl+V. The picture is processed only in your browser and never uploaded to any server.
          2. Pick a mode. Quick looks at the picture first, then suggests the size, the number of colours, the sampling and the background; Pro lets you set each one and recomputes after every change.
          3. Read the pattern. Hold Ctrl and scroll to zoom, and click Fit to see the whole pattern again; click a legend row to isolate that colour, and turn on Compare to check the subject against the original.
          4. Check the counts. The counts per colour add up to the total. Boards are counted as ceil(columns / 29) × ceil(rows / 29), and the finished size is cells × bead size.
          5. Export. The PNG has the grid and legend and prints well, the CSV helps you weigh out beads, the JSON is for further editing, and the print page is laid out for A4.

          About the colour codes

          The built-in codes come from common bead charts, and the screen colours are approximations. Before you buy beads, compare them with the beads you have and adjust the hex values under Palette, or import your own palette as code,name,hex.

          The Basic 29 palette numbers its colours after Hama's colour chart only to make matching beads easy to buy. This app is neither affiliated with nor endorsed by Hama; Hama is a trademark of its owner.

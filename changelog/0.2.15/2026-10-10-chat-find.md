# A find bar searches the conversation, the Session list and the Files panel

- **Date:** 2026-10-10
- **Type:** feature
- **Scope:** `web`, `ui`, `docs`
- **PR:** [#992](https://github.com/Prism-Shadow/penguin-harness/pull/992)
- **Issue:** [#991](https://github.com/Prism-Shadow/penguin-harness/issues/991)

[中文版](2026-10-10-chat-find.zh.md)

The Web App and the desktop app gained a find bar. `find.open` (Mod+F: Ctrl+F, or ⌘F on macOS) searches the area that holds the focus, falling back to the conversation; `find.all` (Mod+Shift+F) searches every area on screen at once and lists the matches. Matching runs over the rendered page in the browser and sends nothing to the server.

## Details

- The searchable areas are the conversation, a subagent's conversation in the Agents panel, the sidebar's Session list and the Files panel. Each declares itself with `data-find-region`; `SidebarFrame` and `FileTree` in `@prismshadow/penguin-ui` gained an optional `findRegion` prop for it.
- `find.open` and `find.all` joined the shortcut registry in the General group, rebindable under System settings › Keyboard shortcuts. Mod+F takes over the browser's own find on purpose, as `editor.save` takes over Save Page; on macOS ⇧⌘F is also Firefox's full screen. Both commands stand down while a dialog or a menu is open, and when the focus is in no area and no conversation is on screen, so the browser keeps its find there.
- Matches are painted through the CSS Custom Highlight API (`::highlight(penguin-find)`, `::highlight(penguin-find-current)`), without changing the DOM.
- Typing updates the count without scrolling. Enter shows the selected match and then steps to the next, Shift+Enter steps back, `Aa` matches case, and Escape inside the bar closes it.
- Once the query settles, collapsed step groups in the searched area open so that the steps listed in them can be found; closing the bar or clearing the query folds them back. A step's own output stays folded.
- `find.all` lists up to 50 matches with their area and the text around them; a button switches between one area and all of them.
- When the searched conversation has earlier turns not yet loaded, the bar offers **Load and keep searching**: it loads the previous window and lands on the nearest match above the one the reader was on.
- The Chat page of the docs gained a "Find in the conversation" section and the two keys in its shortcut table.
- The e2e mock LLM recognizes the title request again by core's title prompt, so the title fixture answers it.

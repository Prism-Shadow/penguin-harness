# The OpenRouter GLM-5.3 Flash row names the promotion it is priced at

- **Date:** 2026-09-16
- **Type:** fix
- **Scope:** `model-catalog`
- **PR:** [#745](https://github.com/Prism-Shadow/penguin-harness/pull/745)

[中文版](2026-09-16-openrouter-glm-5.3-flash-note.zh.md)

The note on OpenRouter's `z-ai/glm-5.3-flash` row attributed its half price to Z.AI's own promotion through 2026-09-09 and said to restore the list price when that ended. Z.AI's promotion has ended, but the row's price is still what OpenRouter's default endpoint bills: DeepInfra (fp4) runs its own 50% discount. The note now names that endpoint, records the 2026-09-16 re-read, and keeps the list rates to restore when DeepInfra's promotion ends. The stored price is unchanged.

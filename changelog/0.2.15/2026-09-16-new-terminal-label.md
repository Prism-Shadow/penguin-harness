# The terminal's "New shell" action reads "New terminal"

- **Date:** 2026-09-16
- **Type:** fix
- **Scope:** `web`
- **PR:** [#746](https://github.com/Prism-Shadow/penguin-harness/pull/746)

[中文版](2026-09-16-new-terminal-label.zh.md)

The action that starts another terminal, in the dock's add-tab menu and on the standalone terminal
page, was labelled "新建 Shell" in Chinese and "New shell" in English while every other terminal
label says terminal. It now reads "新建终端" and "New terminal".

# RFCs may carry an Invariants section

- **Date:** 2026-10-26
- **Type:** process
- **Scope:** `rfcs`
- **PR:** [#1310](https://github.com/Prism-Shadow/penguin-harness/pull/1310)

[中文版](2026-10-26-rfc-invariants.zh.md)

PRFC-0022 (RFC Invariants) was added as a draft. An RFC body may carry an optional `## Invariants` section for the properties its design must always keep: each one a `### <name>` heading, one sentence in a blockquote and a short body, retired only by name in a revision of the RFC that declared it. `rfcs/TEMPLATE.md` gained the section, and the RFC index lists PRFC-0022.

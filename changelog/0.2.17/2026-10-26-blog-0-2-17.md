# The 0.2.17 release post and announcement

- **Date:** 2026-10-26
- **Type:** process
- **Scope:** `landing`
- **PR:** [#1324](https://github.com/Prism-Shadow/penguin-harness/pull/1324)

[中文版](2026-10-26-blog-0-2-17.zh.md)

The blog gained the 0.2.17 release post, in English and Chinese, with screenshots of the app in both
languages. The screenshots live in the community repository's `blog-assets/`.

## Details

- `penguinharness-0-2-17` is a short post: a lead naming the four changes (the Agent API streaming
  a Session's own records, charts and forms in replies, the rebuilt Trajectories panel, and
  conversations on a remote machine reading that machine), one short section with screenshots for
  each, and three upgrading notes that point to the release notes. It marks one key sentence in the
  lead and in each section.
- The landing's announcement bar leads with the release post in place of the 0.2.16 one, and the
  TokenDance Agent Challenge announcement, whose entry period has ended, was removed.
- `packages/landing/scripts/capture-0217-*.mjs` are the scripts that captured the screenshots
  against a scratch data root and a scripted mock model.
- The landing blog test places the post in the news list, and the screenshots' sizes are recorded
  in `blog-image-sizes.json`.

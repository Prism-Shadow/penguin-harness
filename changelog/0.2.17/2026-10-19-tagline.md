# The tagline reads "Fully automatic optimization: any model · any algorithm · any benchmark"

- **Date:** 2026-10-19
- **Type:** process
- **Scope:** `landing`, `docs`, `tooling`
- **PR:** [#1215](https://github.com/Prism-Shadow/penguin-harness/pull/1215), [#1251](https://github.com/Prism-Shadow/penguin-harness/pull/1251)

[中文版](2026-10-19-tagline.zh.md)

The line under "Unified and Stable RSI Framework" changed from "Automatically improve model × agent harness" (zh: 全自动优化模型 × Agent Harness) to "Fully automatic optimization: any model · any algorithm · any benchmark" (zh: 全自动优化：任意模型 · 任意算法 · 任意 Benchmark). Where the tagline stands on its own line, a middle dot separates the three items, the same separator as "Linux · Windows · macOS"; inside a sentence they are listed with commas, or enumeration commas in Chinese.

## Details

- **Landing page.** The hero's second line and the social share card (`public/og-cover.png`, re-rendered from `scripts/og-cover.html`) carried the new tagline with its dots, and the meta and Open Graph descriptions carried it as a sentence. On a phone the hero line wraps only after a dot, never inside an item and never before a dot: the Chinese line keeps its words whole, each English "any" stays with its noun, and a no-break space binds each dot to the item before it.
- **READMEs.** The line under the title used the dotted form and kept its bold on the three items; a no-break space before each dot keeps a narrow window from starting a line with one.
- **Docs.** The introduction's opening paragraph and its one-line summary above the three pillars used the new tagline. In running Chinese prose the three items are separated by enumeration commas: 任意模型、任意算法、任意 Benchmark.
- **Package metadata.** The root `package.json` description followed, as a sentence.

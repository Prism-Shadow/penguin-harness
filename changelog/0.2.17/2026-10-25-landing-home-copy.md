(「让软件持续成长，让进化触手可及」), and the grey line under it is now the subtitle "Free and Stable RSI
Framework" (「免费、稳定的 Agent 自进化框架」); the "for free" badge and the "Fully automatic
optimization" tagline are gone. The supported-models row's closing "and more" links to MMSP's model
index in the site's language, behind the same leave-the-site confirmation as the provider logos. The
second showcase row is titled "Observable self-evolution", the third names the state-of-the-art
self-evolution algorithms with "higher accuracy, lower cost", and the one-sentence legal app case is
titled and introduced as A2APP. Everything else on the page keeps its layout, styles, numbers and
media.

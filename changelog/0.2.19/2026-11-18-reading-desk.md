# Reading Desk

- **Date:** 2026-11-18
- **Type:** feature
- **Scope:** `plugins`, `reading-desk`
- **PR:** [#1594](https://github.com/Prism-Shadow/penguin-harness/pull/1594)

[中文版](2026-11-18-reading-desk.zh.md)

Added a Reading Desk workbench to Utilities. Once installed on an Agent, it opens from the Agent's tabs or the App Center and follows the application's English or Chinese language.

The local bookshelf accepts EPUB, DOCX, Markdown and plain text. The bookshelf shows embedded EPUB covers and reading progress, with search by title or author. Continue reading returns to the same paragraph after a change in screen width or text size. Readers can highlight passages, annotate them and download a book's notes as Markdown.

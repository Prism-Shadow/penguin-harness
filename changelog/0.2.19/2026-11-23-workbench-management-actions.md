# Manage an Agent workbench from its row or running page

- **Date:** 2026-11-23
- **Type:** fix
- **Scope:** `web`
- **PR:** [#1774](https://github.com/Prism-Shadow/penguin-harness/pull/1774)

[中文版](2026-11-23-workbench-management-actions.zh.md)

Open More in Agent settings, the right dock or the full-page workbench to review and restore versions, download the current source as an importable ZIP, or remove the workbench from its Agent. Removal identifies the affected workbench and asks for confirmation.

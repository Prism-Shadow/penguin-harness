# Show or hide a workbench in the App Center

- **Date:** 2026-11-22
- **Type:** feature
- **Scope:** `web`
- **PR:** [#1705](https://github.com/Prism-Shadow/penguin-harness/pull/1705)

[中文版](2026-11-22-workbench-visibility-actions.zh.md)

Each workbench in Agent settings offers Hide from App Center or Unhide in App Center. The action updates the shortcut for the Project. Open still launches a hidden workbench, and the sidebar has its own setting under More.

# Keep the conversation toolbar beside the right dock

- **Date:** 2026-11-24
- **Type:** fix
- **Scope:** `ui`, `web`
- **PR:** [#1821](https://github.com/Prism-Shadow/penguin-harness/pull/1821)

[中文版](2026-11-24-session-dock-columns.zh.md)

The right dock spans the Session from top to bottom on wide screens. The Session title, panel buttons, statistics, composer and bottom dock share the conversation column. Resizing the window keeps at least 320 pixels for the conversation when space permits. Drag its divider to resize the panel, or use the fullscreen button to expand it. On smaller screens the panels share one bottom dock.

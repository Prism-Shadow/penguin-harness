# Open the App Center beside a conversation

- **Date:** 2026-11-23
- **Type:** feature
- **Scope:** `web`
- **PR:** [#1741](https://github.com/Prism-Shadow/penguin-harness/pull/1741)

[中文版](2026-11-23-session-app-center-shortcut.zh.md)

Choose **Open App Center** from a conversation’s shortcuts to browse apps alongside the conversation. Opening an app from there keeps it in the same side panel. This replaces the conversation toolbar’s workbench directory button.

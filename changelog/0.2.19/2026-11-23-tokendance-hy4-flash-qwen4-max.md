# Hy4 Flash and Qwen4 Max on TokenDance

- **Date:** 2026-11-23
- **Type:** feature
- **Scope:** `model`, `server`, `docs`
- **PR:** [#1786](https://github.com/Prism-Shadow/penguin-harness/pull/1786)

[中文版](2026-11-23-tokendance-hy4-flash-qwen4-max.zh.md)

New Projects include `hy4-flash` and `qwen4-max` in the TokenDance group. In an existing Project, use **Add new models** in the model library to add missing presets and keep your current connections. Both models accept images and have a one-million-token context window.

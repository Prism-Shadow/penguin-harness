# Open App Center from the right dock

- **Date:** 2026-11-23
- **Type:** fix
- **Scope:** `web`
- **PR:** [#1764](https://github.com/Prism-Shadow/penguin-harness/pull/1764)

[中文版](2026-11-23-right-dock-app-center.zh.md)

The right dock’s add button offers the same shortcuts as the conversation launcher. App Center fits its controls and app tiles to the width of the dock. Opening an app there keeps it beside the current conversation and draft.

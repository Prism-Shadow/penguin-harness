# Open the first terminal from the conversation dock

- **Date:** 2026-11-24
- **Type:** fix
- **Scope:** `web`, `ui-gallery`
- **PR:** [#1802](https://github.com/Prism-Shadow/penguin-harness/pull/1802)

[中文版](2026-11-24-terminal-first-dock-and-gallery.zh.md)

Choosing Terminal in the conversation dock opens the Session’s existing shell or starts one in its workspace. The terminal’s plus button opens additional shells. In the website demo, the same controls open an in-browser terminal that supports `pwd`, `echo`, `clear` and `help`.

# Show Sessions with open browser pages in App Center

- **Date:** 2026-11-24
- **Type:** fix
- **Scope:** `web`
- **PR:** [#1793](https://github.com/Prism-Shadow/penguin-harness/pull/1793)

[中文版](2026-11-24-open-browser-pages.zh.md)

Browser lists Sessions with open pages, including archived Sessions and subagents. Closing a Session’s last page removes it from the list. To open the first page, choose Browser from that conversation’s shortcuts.

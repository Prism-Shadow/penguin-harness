# Unified site navigation and homepage Agent OS & self-evolution redesign

- **Date:** 2026-11-25
- **Type:** feature
- **Scope:** `ui`, `landing`, `docs`, `ui-gallery`

[中文版](2026-11-25-landing-agent-os-and-evolution-redesign.zh.md)

The public sites (`landing`, `docs`, and `ui-gallery`) now share one `SiteNav` and `SiteMobileNav` implementation from `@prismshadow/penguin-ui/site`, with RFCs grouped inside the More menu. On the homepage, the hero catalogue displays `1000+` supported models, `10+` RSI algorithms, and `20+` Benchmarks under the updated subtitle, the Agent OS section animates app convergence and one-sentence A2APP generation in the App Center, and the Self-Evolution Platform section fuses the optimization workflow with the multi-agent workbench alongside rising accuracy and decreasing cost and duration trends.

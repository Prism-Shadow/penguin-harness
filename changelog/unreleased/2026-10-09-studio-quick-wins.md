# Activity Studio quick wins

- **Date:** 2026-10-09
- **Type:** feat
- **Scope:** `web`

Seven small fixes to the Activity Studio, each one something that got in an author's way.

- **Chat.** The brief the studio sends with a conversation's first message (the activity
  context and the proposal instructions) now folds under the author's question as
  "Context sent to the agent". The author's words show on their own.
- **Chat.** A run's steps read "Worked · 2 steps · 3.7s" in sentence case, in place of the
  uppercase "DONE" label from the chat page. The chat page keeps its own wording.
- **Chat.** The conversation picker gives the time to the minute, as "Today 23:58" or a short
  date. The composer names the section the way the rail does: "About Activity Script", not
  "About Description".
- **Script toolbar.** The diff control is labelled "Compare: Off | Last save | Agent
  proposal". The fold-all button is now "Fold scenes", so it is no longer a second thing
  called "Scenes".
- **Rail.** Clicking a scene's name opens the Activity Script at that scene's heading. Its
  chevron still folds the scene's media.
- **Script colours.** Media uses two hues, violet for what is heard and teal for what is
  seen, with no background wash. A scene full of media no longer reads as one long
  highlight.
- **Panel chips.** Tests shows how many criteria failed, or passed. Quality shows how many
  findings are open. Both show a dot while a run is in progress.

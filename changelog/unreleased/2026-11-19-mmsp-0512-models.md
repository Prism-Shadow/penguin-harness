# Gemini 4 Neon, Gemini Nano Banana 2.1 and MMSP 0.5.12

- **Date:** 2026-11-19
- **Type:** feature
- **Scope:** `model-catalog`, `core`, `server`, `cli`, `docs`
- **PR:** [#1613](https://github.com/Prism-Shadow/penguin-harness/pull/1613)

[简体中文](./2026-11-19-mmsp-0512-models.zh.md)

The model library includes Gemini 4 Neon and Gemini Nano Banana 2.1 from Google and OpenRouter, and PenguinHarness now uses [MMSP 0.5.12](https://github.com/Prism-Shadow/model-message-stream-protocol/releases/tag/v0.5.12). In an existing project, select **Add new models** to add the presets. Your current model selections stay in place.

## Gemini 4 Neon

- Google's `gemini-4-neon` and OpenRouter's `google/gemini-4-neon` accept images and have a 1,048,576-token context window.
- Both list $3 input, $15 output and $0.15 cached input per million tokens, with no promotion.
- On Google's official client, `none` runs at `low` and `max` at `xhigh`. `low`, `medium`, `high` and `xhigh` are sent as picked.

## Gemini Nano Banana 2.1

- Google’s `gemini-nano-banana-2.1` accepts text, images and video through the Interactions API. Its input limit is 131,072 tokens.
- OpenRouter’s `google/gemini-nano-banana-2.1` takes a text prompt through the Images API and generates 1K or 2K images.
- The published rates per million tokens are $1.50 for input, $7.50 for text or thinking output, and $30 for image output. The image estimate uses the $30 image output rate for the whole response and prices separately reported thinking at $7.50. Ordinary response text can make the estimate higher than the invoice.

## MMSP 0.5.12

- A model that MMSP does not recognise gets the thinking levels that every model of its vendor's newest generation accepts. Gemini 2.5 and GPT-5 Mini, which answered the old default with a 400, now work when you add them by hand.
- MiniMax M2.x runs `none` at `low`.

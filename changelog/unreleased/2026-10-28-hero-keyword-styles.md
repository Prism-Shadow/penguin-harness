# Distinct emphasis for the home page slogan

- **Date:** 2026-10-28
- **Type:** feature
- **Scope:** `landing`
- **PR:** [#1375](https://github.com/Prism-Shadow/penguin-harness/pull/1375)

[中文版](2026-10-28-hero-keyword-styles.zh.md)

The home page slogan emphasized “keeps growing” and “within reach” with a soft blue highlight that sweeps through the letters and an outlined airplane in side profile that flies horizontally to the right. Its tail stays attached to the underline, which visibly grows from short to long below the letters before both fade together. Each pass takes about two seconds. Their growth and visibility share one animation so the line appears while it is still growing. The two phrases alternate their emphasis while keeping the surrounding text's size, weight and baseline. The same treatment was applied in Chinese, with colors adjusted for light and dark themes, clauses kept together where space permits, and a still version for reduced motion.

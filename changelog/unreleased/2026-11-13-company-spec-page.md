# The landing site gets a draft spec for the next company mode at /company, with a prototype to click through

- **Date:** 2026-11-13
- **Type:** feature
- **Scope:** `landing`, `ui-gallery`

[中文版](2026-11-13-company-spec-page.zh.md)

penguin.ooo/company is a draft spec for the next company mode. Its main exhibit is a prototype of the mode, built of the Web App's own shell and controls on sample data, framed live on the page: a person talks to one lead agent, work is kept as tickets, each ticket is done by a work session of its own, and a ticket reaches the person only to be accepted, answered or reviewed. Under the prototype the page lists what changes against the shipped mode, what the server enforces, and the ticket file field by field. The shipped company mode is unchanged, and the page links to its docs. The page has no entry in the navigation or the footer; it is reached by its address.

## Details

- **Prototype.** A fourth gallery document, `company.html` (`packages/ui-gallery/src/company`), mounted under the app's own providers like the library boards. The sidebar pins the inbox, the lead and the board, folds the four pages the spec leaves alone, and lists only the work sessions that are running. The inbox takes the overview's place: one line for what is in progress and what it has cost, a filter over the reader's own views (waiting, in progress, later, handled, archived), and the schedules under it, each with a switch that lets its tickets start work directly. A ticket opens in the shipped dialog's frame; where the shipped dialog shows a blocked reason, a ticket that waits on the reader takes the answer in place: accept or reject a proposal, pick an option of its one question, accept a delivery or send it back with a note. The lead's conversation files what the reader says as a ticket. A company with nothing yet asks one sentence, and the lead answers with proposed tickets. No agent runs: a bar under the page moves every work session one step when the reader asks.
- **Rules in the prototype.** Its state (`model.ts`) keeps the spec's rules: an action is taken only on a ticket that waits for it; a ticket turns unread only when a step leaves it waiting, which also brings it back from "later"; a work session stops on its question until it is answered; a schedule's ticket gets no session before it is accepted, unless that schedule was switched to start work directly; a ticket that waits cannot be set aside or archived. `test/company-model.test.ts` holds it to them.
- **The page.** The opening says what the page is, then frames the prototype in the site's language and mode; the window bar chooses the state it opens in (a company a week in, or one before its first sentence) and links to the prototype on its own. Then three sections: what changes against the shipped mode, as rows of the matter, today and the spec; the rules the server enforces, numbered, each with the answer a refused write gets and whether it is enforced today; and the ticket file field by field, with the three frontmatter fields (`source`, `ask`, `deliverables`), the `## Decisions` section and the three per-person triage values (`unread`, `snoozed_until`, `archived`) the spec adds. "Waiting on a person" is read from the five shipped statuses: `proposed`, `review`, or `in_progress` with a person in `blocked_by`.
- **One place per kind of content.** The statuses, the waiting rule, the inbox views, the fields and the rules are in the gallery's `src/company/spec.ts`, which the prototype acts on and the page tables.
- **Shared frame.** The landing's live app frame is split: `LiveFrame` frames any gallery document (lazy, scaled to the column, a guest of the page) and `LiveAppFrame` keeps framing an app surface through it. The home page's frames are unchanged.
- **Site.** The page is its own chunk, loaded on demand. Its tab title and description follow the reader's language; its static shell carries its own title and description with the site's share card. `/company/` is in the sitemap.

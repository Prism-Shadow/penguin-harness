# Open Session tools from the conversation and App Center

- **Date:** 2026-11-23
- **Type:** feat
- **Scope:** Web Session workbenches
- **PR:** [#1736](https://github.com/Prism-Shadow/penguin-harness/pull/1736)

[中文版](2026-11-23-session-shortcut-workbenches.zh.md)

Open Files, Terminal, or Browser from App Center to choose a Session across the Project, including archived conversations and subagents. Terminal and Browser show active resource counts and let you close resources for selected Sessions. Conversation shortcuts open the existing workbench beside their originating Session.

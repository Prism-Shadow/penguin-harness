# The AMSP mark takes a light tone beside MMSP's

- **Date:** 2026-10-26
- **Type:** fix
- **Scope:** `landing`

[中文版](2026-10-26-amsp-mark-tone.zh.md)

The AMSP mark no longer borrows MMSP's near-black tiles. The two tiles MMSP draws near-black are now a light tint of the brand blue (`#aecbfa`) with deep-blue initials (`#0842a0`), and the mark looks the same in the light and dark themes. The blue tiles with their white initials, the shape and the diagonal are unchanged. The new tone shows in the /amsp page's hero and in its share card, `og-amsp.png`, which was re-rendered.

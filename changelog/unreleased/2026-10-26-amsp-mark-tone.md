# The AMSP mark turns two-shade blue beside MMSP's

- **Date:** 2026-10-26
- **Type:** fix
- **Scope:** `landing`
- **PR:** [#1329](https://github.com/Prism-Shadow/penguin-harness/pull/1329)

[中文版](2026-10-26-amsp-mark-tone.zh.md)

The AMSP mark no longer borrows MMSP's near-black tiles. The two tiles MMSP draws near-black are now a deep shade of the brand blue (`#0b57d0`) and the other two take the brand blue `#4285f4`, so beside MMSP's blue and black the mark is two shades of one blue and stays in the site's single colour family. All four initials stay white, and the mark looks the same in the light and dark themes. The shape and the diagonal are unchanged. The new colours show in the /amsp page's hero and in its share card, `og-amsp.png`, which was re-rendered.

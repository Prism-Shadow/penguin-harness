# Dissolve App Center folders with one app

- **Date:** 2026-11-24
- **Type:** fix
- **Scope:** Web App, Server

[中文版](2026-11-24-app-center-folder-membership.zh.md)

When a folder has one app left, that app takes the folder’s position. Empty folders disappear. A temporarily unavailable app keeps its membership. To group apps, choose New folder and select its members, or drag one app onto another.

# Software Update shows how to recover when an update stops responding

- **Date:** 2026-10-16
- **Type:** fix
- **Scope:** `web`

[中文版](2026-10-16-update-recovery-hint.zh.md)

The Software Update section in About now includes a reminder to restart manually or download
the installer from the official website and reinstall if the automatic update stops responding.
The reminder stays visible while downloading and restarting, in both Chinese and English.

# Choose which workbenches appear in Chat

- **Date:** 2026-11-19
- **Type:** feat
- **Scope:** `server`, `web`

[中文](2026-11-19-workbench-chat-tabs.zh.md)

Under an Agent’s settings, open **Plugins → Workbenches** and turn off **Show in chat tabs** for a workbench you use from the App Center. Chat skips its source and pages. The App Center tile and full-screen view can still open it. Each Agent keeps its own selection across restarts and workbench updates. Existing installations start with their chat tabs visible.

# A2UI forms: every basic control, one answer rule, and no more checks on plain text

- **Date:** 2026-10-20
- **Type:** feature
- **Scope:** `core`, `ui`, `web`, `ui-gallery`, `skills`, `docs`
- **PR:** [#1253](https://github.com/Prism-Shadow/penguin-harness/pull/1253), [#1255](https://github.com/Prism-Shadow/penguin-harness/pull/1255)

[中文版](2026-10-20-a2ui-forms.zh.md)

A form in a reply can ask with every basic control: one or several lines of text, a number, a slider, one or several options, a dropdown, a switch, a date, a time, or both. Whether an answer is valid is one rule, shared by the fill button, the line under each control and the saved drafts. The checker no longer checks the text outside the blocks.

## Forms

- Eleven field kinds: `text`, `textarea`, `number`, `slider`, `single`, `multiple`, `select` (a dropdown of 2–30 options), `switch`, `date`, `time` and `datetime`. A slider needs `min` and `max`.
- New field options: `hint` (the line under the control, which is also the error line), `maxLength` (text up to 500, textarea up to 2000), `pattern` (text only, matched against the whole answer) and `default` (the starting answer, typed by kind).
- New checker codes: `not_for_kind` replaces `number_only` for any field placed on a kind that does not read it, and `default_invalid` refuses a default that breaks its own field's rule. A `placeholder` on a kind without one, and a `description` on a dropdown's option, warn and are dropped.
- The fill text keeps one `label: answer` line per field. A switch fills `Yes` / `No` (是 / 否), a number or a slider its figure with the unit, and a datetime its day and time with a space. Text of several lines starts on the next line, each line indented two spaces. A switch and a slider always hold a value, so they always fill a line.
- As text (CLI, messaging channels), each field's bullet says what it takes: its options, range, format or yes / no, followed by its default and its hint.

## Rendering

- The form's button stays off until every required field is answered and every answer is valid. While an answer breaks its rule, the control takes the danger colour and the line under it turns into the error: the field's hint, or the rule in symbols such as `1–10` or `YYYY-MM-DD`.
- New controls in the UI package: `ChoiceGroup` (options as chips or cards), `NumberInput` (minus and plus buttons, the unit inside the box), `Slider`, `FieldLegend` and `RadioDisc`. The gallery shows them under Inputs and Pickers, with number and date-and-time inputs.
- The choice and form blocks are built on the package's controls (`ChoiceGroup`, `NumberInput`, `Slider`, `Select`, `Textarea`, `ToggleRow`) instead of their own styling, so they follow the three themes.

## Checker

- The checks on the prose outside the blocks are removed: long sentences and paragraphs, passive voice, vague words, deep lists and walls of text. The report has two sections, errors and block warnings, and the rubric loses its naturalness question.
- The total is the L2 score when there is no error, and the gate stays at 70, so a reply passes with at most two block warnings.

## Skill and docs

- The `a2ui` skill (version `2026.10.20.2`) teaches the eleven kinds: the catalog row, a kinds table with each control's answer and fill line, the rules for picking a kind, and a worked form with a dropdown, a slider, a date and a switch. Its instructions no longer mention prose checks or a prose score. The writing rules stay as advice, so the skill's one-line description is unchanged.
- Chat › Rich output blocks describes the form's controls and its validation, and the plugin library's README names them.

# Trim narration from the keyboard, and hear what a trim keeps

- **Date:** 2026-09-23
- **Type:** feat
- **Scope:** `web`

The waveform trim in the asset editor now works without a mouse. With the waveform
focused, the arrow keys move the playhead a tenth of a second at a time, or a second with
Shift, and Home and End go to either end. Enter marks where a selection starts, then where
it ends; Escape clears it.

With a stretch selected, the editor says how long the clip would be without it, and
**Play remaining** plays the clip with the selection skipped, so a cut can be heard before
it is made.

# Gemini 3.8 Flash in the preset catalog

- **Date:** 2026-09-03
- **Type:** feat
- **Scope:** `core`, `docs`, `plugins`

[中文版](2026-09-03-gemini-3.8-flash.zh.md)

The preset catalog gains Gemini 3.8 Flash twice: directly on Google with your own key, and on
OpenRouter as `google/gemini-3.8-flash`. Both rows carry a 1,048,576-token context window and
accept images.

Both also store Google's $0.15/$1.50/$7.50 list price and declare its launch discount as
`discount: 0.5`, so a Project is preset with — and the cost center bills — the $0.075/$0.75/$3.75
either seller charges through 2026-12-31, while the price to return to afterwards stays on the
row. That is one field to delete when the promotion lapses, rather than three numbers to
reconstruct. The older `gemini-3.7-flash` rows, whose published pricing is identical, predate
this treatment: the direct row leaves the same discount out and the OpenRouter row has its
price rewritten instead.

The `unified-llm-api` skill's model table lists the new generation alongside its OpenRouter
variant.

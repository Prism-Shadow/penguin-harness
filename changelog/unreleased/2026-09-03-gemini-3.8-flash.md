# Gemini 3.8 Flash in the preset catalog, and the launch discount on every row that carries it

- **Date:** 2026-09-03
- **Type:** feat
- **Scope:** `core`, `docs`, `plugins`
- **PR:** [#603](https://github.com/Prism-Shadow/penguin-harness/pull/603)

[中文版](2026-09-03-gemini-3.8-flash.zh.md)

The preset catalog gains Gemini 3.8 Flash twice: directly on Google with your own key, and on
OpenRouter as `google/gemini-3.8-flash`. Both rows carry a 1,048,576-token context window and
accept images.

Every Gemini 3.x Flash generation Google runs a launch discount on — 3.6, 3.7 and 3.8, directly
and on OpenRouter — now stores the $0.15/$1.50/$7.50 list price and declares that discount as
`discount: 0.5`. A Project is preset with, and the cost center bills, the $0.075/$0.75/$3.75
either seller charges through 2026-12-31, while the price to return to afterwards stays on the
row: one field to delete when the promotion lapses, rather than three numbers to reconstruct.
Two rows were also billing the wrong figure. The OpenRouter `google/gemini-3.7-flash` row had
the gateway's deeper 75%-off promotion baked into its numbers, and that promotion has since
ended, so the row under-billed by half; the OpenRouter `google/gemini-3.6-flash` row stored the
list price while the gateway charged half of it. Both now bill what the gateway bills today.

The `unified-llm-api` skill's model table lists the new generation alongside its OpenRouter
variant.

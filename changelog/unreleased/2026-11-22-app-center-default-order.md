# Default order of App Center tiles

- **Date:** 2026-11-22
- **Type:** fix
- **Scope:** `web`

[中文](2026-11-22-app-center-default-order.zh.md)

The default App Center starts with Models, Plugins, Machines (for administrators), Cost Center and Self-Evolution Workbench. Additional system apps follow them in their declared order. Saved tile and folder positions take precedence.

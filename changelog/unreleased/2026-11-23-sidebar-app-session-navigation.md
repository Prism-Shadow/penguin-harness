- **Date:** 2026-11-23
- **Type:** change

[中文版](2026-11-23-sidebar-app-session-navigation.zh.md)

Workspaces lists conversations and Apps lists the applications open in the current window. Open a conversation or app beside the current one from its row menu. Each split pane shows its current title and controls to swap sides or return to a full page.

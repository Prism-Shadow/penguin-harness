# Manage running apps from the sidebar

- **Date:** 2026-11-23
- **Type:** feature
- **Scope:** `web`, `ui`
- **PR:** https://github.com/Prism-Shadow/penguin-harness/pull/1731

[中文版](2026-11-23-app-window-controls.zh.md)

Running apps have three controls next to their names in the sidebar: More actions, Open full screen, and Close. They are directly accessible with a keyboard or touchscreen. App Center tiles and folders keep their build and update indicators. The green running underline has been removed.

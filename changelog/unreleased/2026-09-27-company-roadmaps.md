# Company roadmaps: a discussion settles into a roadmap that delegates its proposals

- **Date:** 2026-09-27
- **Type:** feature
- **Scope:** `server`, `web`, `plugins`

[中文版](2026-09-27-company-roadmaps.zh.md)

Company mode gained a **roadmap**: what a discussion among several employees settles into — a body written as a paper, and the proposals (each with a brief and an owner, stacked on one another) and roadmaps it leads to. It ships as a module plugin that is off by default, `@prismshadow/penguin-plugin-company-roadmaps` (`plugins/company-roadmaps`), and depends on company-proposals ([2026-09-21-company-proposals.md](2026-09-21-company-proposals.md)): a delegated proposal is created there by its owner.

## Details

- **The room is a channel nobody else sees.** Opening a roadmap opens its room: an organization channel `roadmap_<n>` with the person who opened it and the employees picked (the first one moderates), made through a new organization-gateway call, `OrgGateway.openRoom`, and marked **unlisted** — a new, optional `unlisted = true` in its `channel.toml`, which the channel listing (the sidebar, `GET …/channels`, `penguin org channel ls`) skips. It is reached from the roadmap: the roadmaps page shows the room beside the roadmap (below), and the app's own channel page is one link from there; everything else — reading, posting, inviting and removing members, mentions, archiving — is the channel's own, by its id. For an organization that runs on another machine, `openRoom` is refused here (409 `org_runs_elsewhere`, as the organization routes refuse a write there): this server holds only a mirror of it, and the next copy from the machine would remove the room again — which is how a room opened on such a server ended as "Channel does not exist: roadmap_1". The plugin reads the room's members and messages from disk and never writes to it. A roadmap can still be opened over an existing channel through the API (`channelId`); a channel written before `unlisted` existed reads as listed.
- **An employee's desk, cloned for the room.** For every employee in the room the plugin opens a session of that employee's Agent through the organization gateway, started on the room (who is in it, who moderates, the last 20 messages, how to speak there); every later room message is put into the other members' room sessions through the session runtime, queued behind a running Task, and never reaches a desk. The sessions open when the roadmap is opened, before its answer; in a room a person opened, the moderator's session is told to speak first, to that person (`@user:<id>`) — who is here, the topic, and what they want from the roadmap — and the others' to hold back until one of the two speaks to them. Each opening employee's desk gets one line at the opening: the room, who moderates, and the room session that takes part (nothing to do from the desk); one that cannot be delivered is recorded and returned as a hint. An employee invited in gets a room session at the next pass (every `pollSeconds`, default 5); a removed one has its session closed; a deleted session is opened again. A room session's replies travel at the organization's hop 1, so the plugin keeps its own depth: a person's message is 0, a reply one more than what it answers, and a message at `relayDepth` (default 3) is relayed to no one. A paused organization is not relayed to.
- **A channel a plugin claims wakes no desk.** The organization runtime gained one seam, `OrganizationModule.channelClaims` (typed beside the gateway as `OrgGatewaySlots`): before its pass delivers a channel's mentions to desks it asks every contributed claim, and a channel any claim answers `true` for keeps its messages — recorded, published, read as in any channel — while no mention in it wakes a desk. A claim that throws is recorded (`org_channel_claim_failed`) and counts as not claiming. company-roadmaps claims the room of a roadmap under discussion (not an established or shelved one), from a node of its own that reads the ledger file, and relays the message to the room sessions at once.
- **An entry after the handbook.** Company mode's sidebar shows a **Roadmaps** row (路线图) after the organization's pages, above Proposals; it opens `/org/<project>/<org>/roadmaps`, a page company-roadmaps serves itself (`GET /api/company-roadmaps/page`, in an iframe): the roadmaps with their status, room, items and owners, and one roadmap's record and body, in the app's language. The page is never a blank pane: it is served already saying what it is, says what it is asking before the answer comes, turns a refusal into its HTTP status and the server's message and an answer that has not come in 15 s into a line of its own; and above the list (empty or not) an **Open a roadmap** button unfolds a form — a name and one or more of the organization's employees (from its chart; the first one picked moderates) — that sends `POST …/roadmaps` and goes straight to the new roadmap; a refusal keeps the form and shows the HTTP status and the server's message. The dialog is drawn once: picking an employee moves the moderator's mark and nothing else, so the list keeps its scroll and nothing in the dialog is drawn or animated again. One roadmap is its **room beside its detail**: on the left the room — its messages, read from the channel's own `GET …/channels/<id>/messages` and again every 4 s, and a box that sends with `POST …/messages` (Enter sends, Shift+Enter breaks the line) — and on the right the record, the body and the items; under 900 px they stack, the room first. For an organization that runs on another machine the page asks that machine, as the app does: it reads the Project's organization list once and sends every request through `/server/<machine>/…`, so the plugin must be installed there too (a bare 404 from there says so). No endpoint was added for it. The page wears the app's look: it links the app's stylesheet for framed pages (`/workflow-ui.css`), copies the app's resolved theme values (dark or light, the gray scale, the accent, the font and the root font size) from the window around it and follows them when they change, and lays itself out as the app's company-mode pages do — a header with its primary action, rows in a bordered list with status pills and **Enter the room** (which opens the roadmap: its room and its detail), a centred empty state, a dialog for opening a roadmap (Esc, the backdrop or Cancel close it; **Open** stays disabled until the form is complete), whole rows that open their roadmap, and a **Retry** on a failed read. The web app draws an org-nav row for an iframe page whose key it has a label and a glyph for (`roadmaps`), and orders the contributed rows by its own table of them rather than by contribution order, which follows the Project's `[plugins]` list; the page contribution's shape is unchanged.
- **The draft.** The moderator or a person writes the record, the body and the items (`PUT …/roadmaps/:n/draft`); items are only briefs, and nothing is created while the room discusses. A person may open a roadmap derived from another (`parent`) to continue a discussion in another room; an employee cannot open one.
- **Establishing** (`POST …/:n/establish`, the moderator or a person) needs a body, at least one item and every item's cites naming a heading of the body (400 `cite_unknown` otherwise). It archives the roadmap, stops the relay, and delegates every item at once: a proposal item is a line on its owner's desk with the brief, what it is stacked on (the previous proposal item unless `stackedOn` says otherwise) and the commands to create it with `penguin org proposal create` and link its number back (`POST …/:n/items/:key/link`), after which the owners stacked on it are told the number; a roadmap item becomes a derived roadmap with a room of its own, discussing at once, and its moderator is told the room is open; only when no room can be opened does it wait for one, and its moderator is asked to open one and bind it (`POST …/:n/room`). A line that cannot be delivered is recorded as `notify_failed` and returned as a hint. Established again after a reopening, only the items whose owner or brief changed are delegated again.
- **Reopening** (`POST …/:n/reopen { reason }`, an owner, an employee of the room or a person) puts the roadmap back to discussing and the reason into every room session. A roadmap can be renamed, and a discussion shelved and taken back (`archive` / `unarchive`).
- **Boundaries.** A room session's cost is not counted in the organization's budget, as with any session opened through the gateway. The channel page itself does not show the draft (the roadmaps page shows the two side by side); it can read `GET …/roadmaps?channel=<id>`. Nothing enforces what the opening's desk line asks: a desk that speaks in the room anyway is not stopped.
- The ledger is `<orgDir>/roadmaps.jsonl`, append-only and replayed on first use; `<orgDir>/roadmaps-relay.json` holds each room's cursor and the relay depths.

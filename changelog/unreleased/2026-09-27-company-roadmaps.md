# Company roadmaps: a discussion settles into a roadmap that delegates its proposals

- **Date:** 2026-09-27
- **Type:** feature
- **Scope:** `server`, `web`, `plugins`

[中文版](2026-09-27-company-roadmaps.zh.md)

Company mode gained a **roadmap**: what a discussion among several employees settles into — a body written as a paper, and the proposals (each with a brief and an owner, stacked on one another) and roadmaps it leads to. It ships as a module plugin that is off by default, `@prismshadow/penguin-plugin-company-roadmaps` (`plugins/company-roadmaps`), and depends on company-proposals ([2026-09-21-company-proposals.md](2026-09-21-company-proposals.md)): a delegated proposal is created there by its owner.

## Details

- **The room is a channel nobody else sees.** Opening a roadmap opens its room: an organization channel `roadmap_<n>` with the person who opened it and the employees picked (the first one moderates), made through a new organization-gateway call, `OrgGateway.openRoom`, and marked **unlisted** — a new, optional `unlisted = true` in its `channel.toml`, which the channel listing (the sidebar, `GET …/channels`, `penguin org channel ls`) skips. It is reached from the roadmap (the sidebar's ROADMAPS section and the roadmaps page, below) and read on the app's own channel page, which shows the roadmap beside the stream; everything else — reading, posting, inviting and removing members, mentions, archiving — is the channel's own, by its id. For an organization that runs on another machine, `openRoom` is refused here (409 `org_runs_elsewhere`, as the organization routes refuse a write there): this server holds only a mirror of it, and the next copy from the machine would remove the room again — which is how a room opened on such a server ended as "Channel does not exist: roadmap_1". The plugin reads the room's members and messages from disk and never writes to it. A roadmap can still be opened over an existing channel through the API (`channelId`); a channel written before `unlisted` existed reads as listed.
- **An employee's desk, cloned for the room.** For every employee in the room the plugin opens a session of that employee's Agent through the organization gateway, started on the room (who is in it, who moderates, the last 20 messages, how to speak there); every later room message is put into the other members' room sessions through the session runtime, queued behind a running Task, and never reaches a desk. The sessions open when the roadmap is opened, before its answer; in a room a person opened, the moderator's session is told to speak first, to that person (`@user:<id>`) — who is here, the topic, and what they want from the roadmap — and the others' to hold back until one of the two speaks to them. Each opening employee's desk gets one line at the opening: the room, who moderates, and the room session that takes part (nothing to do from the desk); one that cannot be delivered is recorded and returned as a hint. An employee invited in gets a room session at the next pass (every `pollSeconds`, default 5); a removed one has its session closed; a deleted session is opened again. A room session's replies travel at the organization's hop 1, so the plugin keeps its own depth: a person's message is 0, a reply one more than what it answers, and a message at `relayDepth` (default 3) is relayed to no one. A paused organization is not relayed to.
- **A channel a plugin claims wakes no desk.** The organization runtime gained one seam, `OrganizationModule.channelClaims` (typed beside the gateway as `OrgGatewaySlots`): before its pass delivers a channel's mentions to desks it asks every contributed claim, and a channel any claim answers `true` for keeps its messages — recorded, published, read as in any channel — while no mention in it wakes a desk. A claim that throws is recorded (`org_channel_claim_failed`) and counts as not claiming. company-roadmaps claims the room of a roadmap under discussion (not an established or shelved one), from a node of its own that reads the ledger file, and relays the message to the room sessions at once.
- **ROADMAPS in the sidebar, and the roadmap beside its room.** While company-roadmaps contributes its page, company mode's sidebar has a **ROADMAPS** section below the channel list, apart from "My channels": the five most recently active roadmaps under discussion (by their latest ledger event; shelved, established and room-less ones are left out), the rest behind "Show n more", a **+** that opens the roadmaps page with its dialog up (`?open=1`), and **All roadmaps**. A row opens the roadmap's room — the app's own channel page, whose stream, composer, mentions and phone layout are the channel's — and that page, for a channel that is a roadmap's room (`GET …/roadmaps?channel=<id>`), shows the roadmap in a column on the right: the plugin's page in its detail view (`?view=detail&n=<n>`: record, body, items, re-read every 10 s). On a wide window the two stand side by side; under the `lg` breakpoint the stream keeps the screen and a **Roadmap #n** bar under the header swaps the column in ("Back to the room" swaps it back). The org nav no longer has a Roadmaps row; the page stays at `/org/<project>/<org>/roadmaps`. That page (`GET /api/company-roadmaps/page`, in an iframe) lists the roadmaps one line each, as the proposals list does — number, name, status, room, moderator, how many items and when it last moved; the items themselves (briefs, owners, approvals) are on the roadmap's own detail — in the app's language, and is never a blank pane: it is served already saying what it is, says what it is asking before the answer comes, turns a refusal into its HTTP status and the server's message and an answer that has not come in 15 s into a line of its own. Above the list an **Open a roadmap** button unfolds a form — a name and one or more of the organization's employees (from its chart; the first one picked moderates) — that sends `POST …/roadmaps` and goes straight to the new roadmap's room (it stays on the roadmap first when the server returned hints, such as a room session that could not open); a refusal keeps the form and shows the HTTP status and the server's message. The dialog is drawn once: picking an employee moves the moderator's mark and nothing else, so the list keeps its scroll and nothing in the dialog is drawn or animated again. A row, its **Enter the room** and the dialog's **Open** all go to the room; the page shows a roadmap's detail itself only while it waits for a room. For an organization that runs on another machine the page asks that machine, as the app does: it reads the Project's organization list once and sends every request through `/server/<machine>/…`, so the plugin must be installed there too (a bare 404 from there says so). The page wears the app's look: it links the app's stylesheet for framed pages (`/workflow-ui.css`), copies the app's resolved theme values (dark or light, the gray scale, the accent, the font and the root font size) from the window around it and follows them when they change, and lays itself out as the app's company-mode pages do. The web app reads the roadmaps through `listOrgRoadmaps` (the plugin's own route) and finds the page by its contributed key `roadmaps`; the page contribution's shape is unchanged, and the org nav still orders the contributed rows it draws by its own table rather than by the Project's `[plugins]` order.
- **The draft.** The moderator or a person writes the record, the body and the items (`PUT …/roadmaps/:n/draft`); items are only briefs, and nothing is created while the room discusses. A person may open a roadmap derived from another (`parent`) to continue a discussion in another room; an employee cannot open one.
- **Establishing** (`POST …/:n/establish`, the moderator or a person) needs a body, at least one item and every item's cites naming a heading of the body (400 `cite_unknown` otherwise). It archives the roadmap and stops the relay. A proposal item becomes **a brief and nothing more**: no proposal is created, its owner is not told, and no text tells anyone to create one, until **a person and the moderator have both approved that brief** (`POST …/:n/items/:key/approve`: a person, from the **Approve** button beside the item on the roadmaps page; the moderator, from its room session, which the establishment asks for them; each once, recorded with who and when). With both, its owner's desk gets one line — the item, who approved it and when, what it is stacked on (the previous proposal item unless `stackedOn` says otherwise) — and no command: creating the proposal and linking its number back (`POST …/:n/items/:key/link`, refused with 409 `not_approved` before the approvals) are write steps the organization does where it does writes. The two approvals start the work on a new proposal; an item that is a proposal which exists already (its work often done) has nothing to start, so it is not approved: a person, or a moderator that does not own it, links the brief to that proposal, and it is delegated at once — no approvals, no desk line — while its owner's own link still waits for both; once linked, the owners stacked on it are told the number. A roadmap item becomes a derived roadmap with a room of its own, discussing at once, and its moderator is told the room is open; only when no room can be opened does it wait for one (a person binds one, `POST …/:n/room`), and its moderator's desk is told so, without a command. A line that cannot be delivered is recorded as `notify_failed` and returned as a hint. Established again after a reopening, only the items whose owner or brief changed start again (a changed brief loses its approvals). No desk line the plugin sends carries a write command.
- **An existing proposal taken in.** A proposal that already exists joins a roadmap as it is (`POST …/:n/adopt { proposal, title, owner, brief? }`, the moderator or a person, while the room discusses or once the roadmap is established; on the page, **Add an existing proposal** beside the items offers the organization's proposals that are not in it yet — company-proposals' own list — with the implementer, else the author, as its owner). It is a proposal item carrying its number (`proposal`, no cites needed): nothing is created for it, it needs no approvals and no desk is told — it has its own page for that. An establishment delegates it and links its number at once (at once, too, on an established roadmap); written items stacked on it see that number. The moderator keeps it in the items it writes; the same proposal is refused twice (409 `already_adopted`).
- **Reopening** (`POST …/:n/reopen { reason }`, an owner, an employee of the room or a person) puts the roadmap back to discussing and the reason into every room session. A roadmap can be renamed, and a discussion shelved and taken back (`archive` / `unarchive`).
- **Boundaries.** A room session's cost is not counted in the organization's budget, as with any session opened through the gateway. Nothing enforces what the opening's desk line asks: a desk that speaks in the room anyway is not stopped.
- The ledger is `<orgDir>/roadmaps.jsonl`, append-only and replayed on first use; `<orgDir>/roadmaps-relay.json` holds each room's cursor and the relay depths.

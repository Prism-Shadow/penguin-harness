# Open conversations and apps side by side

- **Date:** 2026-11-22
- **Type:** feature
- **Scope:** `web`, `ui`
- **PR:** https://github.com/Prism-Shadow/penguin-harness/pull/1706

[中文版](2026-11-22-session-app-pages.zh.md)

The sidebar lists the conversations and apps open in this window. Open a page on either side, swap the panes, or give one page the full width. On a phone, select the page to show from the same list.

A conversation can open its Agent’s workbench directory beside the transcript. Each workbench opens as an independent page. The Apps and Workspaces sections fold separately, and the app list supports search and sorting.

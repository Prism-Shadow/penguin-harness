# Looping homepage demo and lighter page activity

- **Date:** 2026-10-28
- **Type:** feature
- **Scope:** `landing`, `ui-gallery`, `ui`
- **PR:** [#1380](https://github.com/Prism-Shadow/penguin-harness/pull/1380)

[中文版](2026-10-28-homepage-loop-and-loading.zh.md)

The homepage's Agent evolution walkthrough repeated after a four-second hold on its score curve. It kept Pause and Play available across rounds, reused the same app window and removed completed automatic Sessions before starting again. Playback waited while the window or browser tab was out of view; manual input still took control.

Three repeated app previews and their descriptions were removed, leaving one live app on the page. The homepage dropped its moving blurred background, delayed loading the app until near the viewport, and stopped offscreen charts, comparisons and case rotation. Supporting facts used a compact three-column layout on phones.

A Back to top button appeared at the lower right after scrolling. It returned focus to the homepage heading and used an immediate scroll under reduced motion.

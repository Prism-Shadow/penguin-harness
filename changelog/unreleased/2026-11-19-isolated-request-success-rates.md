# Cost Center shows isolated success rates

- **Date:** 2026-11-19
- **Type:** fix
- **Scope:** `web`
- **PR:** [#1611](https://github.com/Prism-Shadow/penguin-harness/pull/1611)

[中文版](2026-11-19-isolated-request-success-rates.zh.md)

In the agent and model charts, bars show request counts on the left axis. The right axis shows success rates. A single measured rate gets a point, including at 0% or 100%. Gaps in the data leave a break in the line. Tap a bucket to read its values, or focus the chart and use the arrow keys.

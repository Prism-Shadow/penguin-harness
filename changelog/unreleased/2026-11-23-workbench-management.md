# Manage apps from the Plugin Market

- **Date:** 2026-11-23
- **Type:** change
- **Scope:** `web`

[中文版](2026-11-23-workbench-management.zh.md)

**Manage apps** in the sidebar opens the current Project’s Plugin Market. In Agent settings, a workbench row offers **Enable** and **Open**. Open the workbench’s name to see its files and access reload, version history, export and removal. Enabled workbenches can still be opened here when their App Center tile is hidden.

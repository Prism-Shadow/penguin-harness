# Use the conversation side panel for apps

- **Date:** 2026-11-23
- **Type:** feature
- **Scope:** `web`

[中文版](2026-11-23-chat-app-side-panel.zh.md)

An app opened beside a conversation uses its existing panel tabs and resize handle. The conversation stays on the left. Hiding an app or moving it between the panel and a full page keeps the running workbench.

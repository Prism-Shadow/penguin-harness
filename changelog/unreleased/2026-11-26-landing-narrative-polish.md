# Homepage App Center run, self-evolution loops and model logos

- **Date:** 2026-11-26
- **Type:** feature
- **Scope:** `landing`
- **PR:** [#1872](https://github.com/Prism-Shadow/penguin-harness/pull/1872)

[中文版](2026-11-26-landing-narrative-polish.zh.md)

The homepage App Center scene generated nine apps in a run, one or two building at a time, then rested on the full home screen and started over; every tile, generated or installed, wore an icon instead of a letter. In the self-evolution workbench each tested agent's loop became one arrow turning round a full circle, the connectors from the Optimizer and the Evaluators became one rounded bus with arrowheads on every drop and their labels beside the lines, and a round took two seconds. The supported-model logos in the hero gained TokenDance and OpenCode. The headings, subtitles and card text from the App Center section to the end of the homepage were shortened.

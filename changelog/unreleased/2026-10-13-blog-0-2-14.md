The blog gained the 0.2.14 release post and five tutorials, in English and Chinese, with screenshots
of the app in both languages. The screenshots live in the community repository's `blog-assets/`.

## Details

- `penguinharness-0-2-14` covers the release in three themes: self-evolution (the five RSI toolkits
  and the seven built-in Benchmarks), agents that browse, show UI and build pages (browser
  automation, A2UI, workflows), and running anywhere (remote machines, the Agent API), followed by
  the other changes and what to do when upgrading.
- The tutorials are `self-evolve-agents-with-rsi-toolkits`, `let-agents-use-the-browser`,
  `build-agent-apps-with-workflows`, `run-agents-on-remote-machines` and
  `call-agents-with-the-agent-api`.
- `packages/landing/scripts/capture-0214-*.mjs` are the scripts that captured the screenshots
  against a scratch data root and a scripted mock model.
- The release skill's prose-polishing script defaults to `glm-5.3-flash` on the TokenDance gateway,
  and its guide says what a key without access to a listed model answers.
- The landing blog test counts the new posts and their places in the news and practice lists.

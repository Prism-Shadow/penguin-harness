# Manage open pages from the sidebar

- **Date:** 2026-11-23
- **Type:** feature
- **Scope:** `web`, `ui`

[中文版](2026-11-23-compact-open-pages.zh.md)

The sidebar shows up to six open pages, including both visible panes. Open **All open pages** to search the entire list. Use a row's menu to minimize or restore a page, open an app full screen, or close it. Apps with multiple subpages keep their own page selector above the content.

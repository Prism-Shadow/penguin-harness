# Reduce the new chat's shortcuts to Build New App and Optimize App

- **Date:** 2026-11-25
- **Type:** feature
- **Scope:** `web`, `ui-gallery`
- **PR:** [#1860](https://github.com/Prism-Shadow/penguin-harness/pull/1860)

[中文版](2026-11-25-home-shortcuts-simplify.zh.md)

Replaced the new chat page's shuffled example prompts with two fixed shortcuts, centred under the composer and drawn as pills. Build New App fills a prompt for a new workbench app published to the App Center. Optimize App opens a searchable list of the Project's apps on the draft's machine, system apps excluded, and fills a prompt naming the chosen one; while the list loads, or when it cannot be read, the menu says so and offers a retry. Saved shortcuts follow the two, and Shuffle suggestions and More examples were removed.

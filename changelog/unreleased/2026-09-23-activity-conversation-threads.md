# Conversation threads in the activity studio

- **Date:** 2026-09-23
- **Type:** feature
- **Scope:** `web`

The activity's conversation panel now lists every conversation about the activity, newest
first. Before, it only resumed the newest one.

## Details

- A picker at the top of the panel names each conversation by what the author had open
  when it started and when that was ("About scene intro · …"). It also offers a new
  conversation.
- The proposal the studio shows follows the open conversation: the proposal card, the
  script editor's Agent proposal diff and marks, and the storyboard's marks.

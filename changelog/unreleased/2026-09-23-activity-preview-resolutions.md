# Try an activity at other screen sizes

- **Date:** 2026-09-23
- **Type:** feat
- **Scope:** `web`

The activity player has a resolution picker beside Play. It starts at the activity's own
resolution from its specification, and offers common classroom screens (1024×768,
1280×800, 1366×768, 1920×1080 and 768×1024 portrait) to try the module at. Only the
preview changes; the specification keeps its resolution.

# App Center folders open from their icons

- **Date:** 2026-11-19
- **Type:** feature
- **Scope:** `web`, `ui`

[中文](2026-11-19-app-center-folders.zh.md)

Open a folder in the App Center and its preview icons spread into a grid. The back arrow or Escape folds it back into place. Closing a workbench returns to the folder it came from. With reduced motion enabled, the folder opens and closes immediately.

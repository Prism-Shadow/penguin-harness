# A fourth theme, Doodle, drawn by hand

- **Date:** 2026-11-04
- **Type:** feature
- **Scope:** `ui`, `web`, `ui-gallery`
- **PR:** [#1473](https://github.com/Prism-Shadow/penguin-harness/pull/1473), [#1495](https://github.com/Prism-Shadow/penguin-harness/pull/1495)

[中文版](2026-11-04-theme-cartoon.zh.md)

Settings › Appearance and the gallery's theme switcher gained a fourth theme, Doodle (卡通, id `cartoon`): paper and ink in solid colour, every box edged with a pen line that its fill follows exactly, in light and in dark (chalk on dark paper).

## Details

- The line: each shape — a control, a surface, a pill, a checkbox's box, a tab and a ball — is one rounded outline traced by a pen from a fixed seed in `packages/ui/scripts/draw-doodle-edges.mjs`, its stroke starting a hair inside the box and running out onto the line. From the same points the script draws the pen line, laid as a CSS `border-image` 9-slice — for the shapes a page shows by the dozen a PNG at twice its size through `image-set`, so a box's line is copied rather than redrawn on every repaint (`packages/ui/scripts/raster-strokes.mjs`, no new dependency), for the rest an SVG — and writes the outline as a `clip-path: shape()` whose points stretch as the 9-slice does, so a control's, a bubble's or a notice's own background is clipped to its line at every size and a resize never redraws the wobble. A card's or a code block's fill takes instead the CSS corner the script finds stays under the pen at every corner of the outline; hover, selection, a tone's wash and the accent presets still colour the fill from CSS. Browsers without `shape()` (Chrome before 135, Safari before 18.4, Firefox before 141) keep the fill inside the line's border instead.
- Boxed buttons, text fields, select triggers and the person's own message bubbles take the line through a new style hook, `ui-edge`; cards, code blocks, tool calls, notices and reply widgets through their existing hooks; a host drawn as a capsule or a circle takes the pill. On cards, code blocks, notices and widgets the line is drawn over the box's contents, so a row's or a head strip's own fill ends under it too. A step of the agent's work in a conversation — an activity card or a one-line note — takes a pencil hairline instead of the pen, so a long run is not hundreds of drawn boxes; the bubbles, the composer and the cards round them keep it. The other three themes treat `ui-edge` as a no-op.
- What floats — dialogs, menus, popovers, tooltips, the composer and its slash list — is painted by one image holding its fill, its line and a soft shadow in a 12 px outset, so the shadow follows the drawn shape and no `filter` runs; the composer is redrawn in ballpoint blue while it has focus. The launcher's ball and its fan are drawn round.
- Keyboard focus is drawn inside the line, which a ring outside it would lose to the clip: the theme's ballpoint-blue ring 3 px in from the edge, with a band of the slip inside it on a button so it reads on a filled one, and a focused field redrawn in the blue pen. In forced colours the line, the clip and the painted slips are left out and the host's own border, corners and ring return in the system's colours.
- The script also draws the paper marks the theme's sections place: a highlighter band, a blue pencil bracket and a pencil rule each way.
- The theme's recipes are partials under `packages/ui/src/themes/cartoon/` that `cartoon.css` imports. The token-contract, contrast and de-slop suites read a theme together with its partials and skip the recipes' private `--_*` properties; a Doodle suite checks that the theme's images and clips are the script's, that every one a recipe reads is drawn, and that no `filter` runs outside a chart; the contrast suite measures the focus ring at 3:1 on every surface a focused control sits on.
- Type: Shantell Sans for Latin (`@fontsource-variable/shantell-sans`, SIL OFL 1.1) and LXGW WenKai GB Screen 1.530 for Chinese (SIL OFL 1.1). WenKai ships as the 48 slices of its web release that hold every character of the GB2312 grid (2.8 MB), vendored by `packages/ui/scripts/vendor-wenkai.mjs` from the pinned `lxgw-wenkai-screen-web` tarball; rarer characters fall back to Noto Sans SC. Both faces are credited with their licence texts.
- Accent: the theme's own is black and white like the others; five pen presets (marker, ballpoint, grass, crayon, grape) join the accent picker.
- A theme id the running build does not know (one a newer build synced through the account) keeps showing the default theme and stays stored as it was.
- Five section hooks let a theme dress a whole section its own way: `ui-start` (the new-conversation start page), `ui-library` (the model library's groups and cards), `ui-ledger` (the usage summary cards, the company KPI tiles, a Benchmark card's score), `ui-empty` (an empty state) and `ui-run` (the chat toolbar's run indicator, now `RunStatus`). Badges, checkboxes and radios join `ui-edge`; the spinner and an empty state's art are drawn in every style at once, of which a theme shows its own; the chart patterns and filter (`ChartDefs`) are defined once per page; and a filled chart mark takes its fill from its own paint. The open conversation's sidebar row is marked as the current page for assistive technology. Primer, Frost and Console look exactly as before.
- The running state: a word that says something runs — the toolbar's run indicator, a running work group's title, a tool call's name, a thinking row — is set in ink over a highlighter band; a step in flight draws its progress as a pencil scribble stepping along; the spinner is a pencil spiral; the streaming caret is a short slanted pen stroke; and the selected row on the navigation column (a page or the open conversation) is highlighted across, with a blue pencil bracket in its margin, in place of the grey wash. The band and the bracket are still; the scribble, the spiral and the caret stop under reduced motion.
- The sidebar, the conversation and empty states: the person's own messages are outlined in ballpoint blue, a steer sent into a running Task at a control's size; an activity card's steps hang off its head on pencil lines, a dashed guide past each step and a solid `└` into the last. The navigation column is ruled off from the page by a pencil line, New chat sits in a dashed pencil box and the list's group label trails a short pencil rule; in the phone's drawer the sidebar takes the same marks, its selected row highlighted with the bracket as on the column. An empty state shows a small pencil squiggle above its words, and its slot form is a dashed pencil frame with a tick at each corner.

# Reading Desk

- **Date:** 2026-11-18
- **Type:** feature
- **Scope:** `plugins`, `reading-desk`

[中文版](2026-11-18-reading-desk.zh.md)

Added a Reading Desk workbench to Utilities. Once installed on an Agent, it opens from the Agent's tabs or the App Center and follows the application's English or Chinese language.

The local bookshelf accepts EPUB, DOCX, Markdown and plain text. The reader restores the last chapter and scroll position. Readers can highlight passages, annotate them and download a book's notes as Markdown.

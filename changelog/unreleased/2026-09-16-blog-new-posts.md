- **Date:** 2026-09-16

Three posts were added to the blog in English and Chinese, with screenshots of the Web App in both
languages and diagrams for the concepts.

## Details

- `connect-agents-to-feishu-qq-telegram-wechat` is a tutorial: create a conversation, open
  **Remote control** from its right-click menu, bind it to WeChat by scanning a QR code (including
  the wait before the first WeChat message), talk to the agent from a phone, then bind Feishu,
  Telegram or QQ. It ends with the limits, the safety rules and troubleshooting.
- `create-evaluate-evolve-agents` is a tutorial for the Evaluation Center: have AI create a
  Benchmark and take a baseline, ask AI what a case tests, replace a Benchmark with a new one and
  delete the old one, then evaluate and optimize an agent. Two diagrams show what the Optimizer and
  the Evaluator can read and change, and one optimization round.
- `agent-company-mode` is a perspectives essay on company mode: how it relates to products that put
  people and many agents in one space, what an organization adds, the one-person company and the
  mirror company, and the limits of the beta.
- The landing blog test counts the new posts and their places in the practice and perspectives
  lists.

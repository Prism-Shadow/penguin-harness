# Keep "Hide launcher" out of the dock's plus menu

- **Date:** 2026-11-26
- **Type:** fix
- **Scope:** `web`

[中文版](2026-11-26-hide-launcher-out-of-plus-menu.zh.md)

The dock's "+" menu no longer lists "Hide launcher". The entry stays on the floating launcher's own menu, and a hidden ball is shown again with the Shortcuts launcher toggle in Settings › Appearance.

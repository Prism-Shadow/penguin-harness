# Keep the Session draft when a workbench fills the screen

- **Date:** 2026-11-23
- **Type:** fix
- **Scope:** `web`
- **PR:** [#1756](https://github.com/Prism-Shadow/penguin-harness/pull/1756)

[中文版](2026-11-23-docked-workbench-fullscreen.zh.md)

Opening a docked workbench full screen keeps the conversation and the workbench open. Exiting returns to the same conversation with its unsent message and attached files.

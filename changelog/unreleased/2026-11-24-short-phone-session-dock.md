# Open Session panels on short phone screens

- **Date:** 2026-11-24
- **Type:** fix
- **Scope:** Web App
- **PR:** [#1829](https://github.com/Prism-Shadow/penguin-harness/pull/1829)

On short phone screens, the Session panel picker and its controls stay within reach when the composer contains a long notice or draft. The composer scrolls to show its input and notices while the panel is open.

# Open Session panels on short phone screens

- **Date:** 2026-11-24
- **Type:** fix
- **Scope:** Web App
- **PR:** [#1829](https://github.com/Prism-Shadow/penguin-harness/pull/1829)

On short phone screens, the Session panel picker and its controls stay within reach when the composer contains a long notice or draft. While the keyboard is open, the current line and the panel’s close button stay visible. You can scroll to the composer notices and tap slash commands above the input.

# Workbench window controls fit on a phone

- **Date:** 2026-11-22
- **Type:** fix
- **Scope:** `ui`

[中文版](2026-11-22-workbench-title-bar.zh.md)

In a narrow workbench window, the page switcher sits below the app name and window buttons. Long page lists scroll horizontally inside the switcher. Wider windows show the pages beside the name.

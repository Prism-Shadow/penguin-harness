# State colours follow rounded corners

- **Date:** 2026-11-18
- **Type:** fix
- **Scope:** `ui`, `web`, `ui-gallery`

[中文](2026-11-18-rounded-surfaces.zh.md)

File-drop feedback follows the shape of its target. Switches keep the moving thumb inside the track, and sidebar drop indicators follow the target’s corners. On the new-chat page, a count link’s hover fill follows its tile. Keyboard focus rings and menus can still extend beyond their controls.

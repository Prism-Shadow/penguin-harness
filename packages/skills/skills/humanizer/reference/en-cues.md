# English (en)

Default-AI markers:

- "not just X, but Y" and every correction-shaped frame, including the strawman-swerve ("was not trying to X; he wanted Y" against a position nobody holds).
- Epigram saturation: every paragraph ends quotable; chiasmus and mirrored re-description ("engages with detail / the detail it engages with"); "X dressed up as Y"; zero duds anywhere.
- Rule of three at every scale, including the "vivid concrete triad" with an expanding final item ("the sick week, the flaky test suite, the dependency upgrade that breaks the build").
- Maxim anaphora ("Shipping beats planning. Feedback beats instinct. Done beats perfect.").
- Em-dash saturation — or, after a scrub, em-dash extermination with semicolon inflation.
- Scope-widener transitions ("The implications extend far beyond…"), anadiplosis echo ledes ("Piece by piece was the whole problem.").
- Bookend kickers ("What began as X now Y") and "That, in the end, is…: not A, but B" ring closers.
- Testament-speak and pet abstractions: stands as a testament, tapestry, landscape, inflection point, connective tissue.
- Participial analysis tails ("…, highlighting the challenges facing the sector").
- Vague authority ("experts agree"), hedge inflation ("arguably one of the most"), era openers ("In today's fast-paced world").
- Elegant-variation epithet cycling (the device… the ancient computer… the bronze marvel).
- Mechanical concession pivot ("To be sure, challenges remain. But…") and "remains to be seen" closers.

Scrubbed-text markers: dash and parenthesis blackout in registers that breathe through both; comma splices doing dash work ("did not improve the old system, they replaced it"); first-person amputation in opinion genres; ownerless anecdotes ("The feature," no company, no year); fragments For Effect.

Native, not a tell: the occasional triad, one earned antithesis, semicolons in essay register — at frequencies a human editor would pass.

Print norms: pick one style guide and hold it; "26 April 1956" alongside "aluminum" is a pipeline fingerprint. Parentheses for abbreviations: "(TEU)", not ", TEU."

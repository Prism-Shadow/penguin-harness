# Plugin market cover art

Every cover in the plugin market is drawn here, from one spec, as vector art, and exported to the
WebP files the market serves. Nothing is hand-painted, fetched or generated by an outside service:
the shapes are drawn in code, the glyphs are the plugins' own icons and the product's icon registry,
and the one typeface (the RSI toolkits' lettering) is Mona Sans, the product's own (OFL-1.1, from
`@fontsource-variable/mona-sans`), embedded in the file.

```sh
pnpm install --frozen-lockfile                          # Playwright's Chromium does the export
node scripts/market-cover-art/build.mjs --svg /tmp/covers   # the vector sources, to look at
node scripts/market-cover-art/build.mjs --webp              # the covers this build ships
node scripts/market-cover-art/build.mjs --webp --community ../penguin-harness-community
```

`--webp` writes `cover.webp` and `cover-640.webp` into `packages/server/src/plugin/market-covers/<id>/`
for every plugin the build ships (a package under `plugins/`, or an external built-in the server
pins). `--community` also writes every cover into a community checkout's `assets/plugins/<id>/`,
where the published market document names them, and puts each cover's `tone` and today's `updated`
date into its `market.json` (run prettier on that file afterwards). The community copy is the one
release preparation syncs back into this repository (`scripts/sync-market-assets.mjs`), so a cover
changed here reaches the next release only once the community repository has it too.

| File | What it holds |
| --- | --- |
| `covers.mjs` | The spec: plugin id → template or scene, hue, shade, size |
| `kit.mjs` | The canvas, the palette, the lit field, and the solid objects and their shadows |
| `scenes.mjs` | The templates (`stage`, `emblem`, `lettering`) and one scene per plugin |
| `build.mjs` | Draws the spec, writes SVG and WebP |

## The system

- **Canvas and safe area.** 1600 × 900 (16:9). The subject stays inside the middle of the square a
  1:1 crop keeps (`SAFE` in `kit.mjs`), so the cover survives any crop from 16:9 to 1:1, and reads
  at a 160 px card as well as a 960 px hero. The wings outside that square carry only the field.
  The top-right corner stays clear as well: a card lays its version chip there.
- **One hue per cover.** A cover's colours all derive from one OKLCH hue (`palette` in `kit.mjs`):
  lights lean a few degrees toward yellow and shadows toward blue, so one hue reads as a lit volume.
  Each plugin keeps the hue family of its `tone` in the market document; neighbours that share a
  family differ by shade, not by a new hue.
- **One light.** The field is lit from the upper left, deepens toward the lower right and settles
  into shade at the corners. Every object is lit by the same light: a white-to-tint face, a rim of
  light along its top edge, and a two-layer shadow (a wide soft fall and a tight contact shade) down
  and to the right. Gradients exist only as this light.
- **Objects, not screenshots.** A subject is built from the plugin's own domain objects in paper
  white and the hue's shades: a chart, a target, a shield, a slide deck. No shrunk screenshot, no
  imitation of the product's interface, no words, no scores or counts, no product logo.
- **One corner rule.** App tiles take the home screen's corner (23.5 % of the side); other boxes
  about 12 % of their shorter side. Glyphs keep the app's line weight (1.7 on the 24-unit grid),
  scaled with the glyph.

## Templates

| Template | For | Drawn from |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `scene` | most plugins | a drawing of its own in `SCENES`, built from the kit |
| `emblem` | sandbox backends | a bevelled shield carrying the icon's inner mark, inside dashed bounds, with the shell prompt it confines |
| `lettering` | RSI toolkits | the method's name, large, over the RSI loop: the name is what tells ten toolkits of one family apart |
| `stage` | any plugin, in one line | its icon on a lit app tile |

The generated cover the app draws for a plugin with no picture (`Cover` in `packages/ui`) follows the
same field, light and tile, in CSS.

## Adding a cover

1. Add the plugin to `COVERS` in `covers.mjs`: `stage` needs nothing but a hue family; a scene needs
   a function of the same id in `SCENES`.
2. Add it to the market document (`assets/plugins/market.json` in the community repository) under
   the package's name, with `<id>/cover.webp`, `<id>/cover-640.webp` and the printed `tone`.
3. Export, and look at it before committing: at card size and hero size, on a light and a dark page,
   cropped to 16:9, 4:3 and 1:1, beside its neighbours on the shelf.

## Export rules

- `cover.webp` 1280 × 720 at WebP quality 0.82, `cover-640.webp` 640 × 360 at 0.80, sRGB, encoded by
  Chromium's canvas. The community repository's check fails a file over 150 KB / 45 KB; these covers
  stay far below that, and the build refuses one that does not.
- The SVG source is self-contained: no `<script>`, no external reference, no raster; filters are
  Gaussian blurs only, which Chromium and WebKit draw alike.
